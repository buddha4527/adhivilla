01 · THE PLACE

Thirunelli, with a little more silence.

Adhi Villa is in Sarvani, a quiet rural village about two kilometres from the main Thirunelli area. It is close enough to reach the temple, river, trekking routes and forests easily — but far enough to feel removed from the busy side of the destination.

More privacy. More greenery. More village life. A slower way to experience northern Wayanad.
